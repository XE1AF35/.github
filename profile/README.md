# XE1AF35

Small, local-first tools for photographers.

Everything runs in your browser and stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

## Projects

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Bodycap](https://github.com/XE1AF35/bodycap) | Organise your camera gear: lenses, bodies, kits, loans, and maintenance. Works offline. [Open it](https://xe1af35.github.io/bodycap/) |
| [Hotshoe](https://github.com/XE1AF35/hotshoe) | Log where you took a photo on your iPhone, then geotag it later with GPX or coordinates. *Early development.* |

## Principles

- **Local-first:** your data lives on your device, with backups you control
- **Private by default:** no analytics, no tracking, and nothing sent anywhere
- **Open source:** all projects are MIT licensed
